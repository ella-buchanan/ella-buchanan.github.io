About Us

We are a family-owned coffee shop established in 2016. We are committed to providing the highest quality coffee and food in a warm and welcoming environment. We believe coffee is more than just a drink. It’s a reason to slow down, catch up with a friend, or enjoy a quiet moment in your day. Whether you’re stopping by for your morning latte or staying for an afternoon pick-me-up, we’re happy to have you here. Come visit us and make Little Market part of your day. We can’t wait to welcome you in!
