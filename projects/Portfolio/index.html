Featured Photography

My name is Ella Buchanan, and I am a passionate photographer dedicated to capturing the beauty and essence of life's moments. I am an award-winning sports, events, and portrait photographer trusted to deliver high-impact visuals under pressure.

From capturing peak action on the sidelines as a UMD Athletics Photography Intern to documenting our shared landscapes as the winner of the National Parks Photography Competition 2025, my work blends technical precision with a deep foundation in photography.

Based in the Washington D.C., Maryland, and Virginia region, I partner with athletic departments and creative agencies to deliver premium commercial assets, while working locally with individuals and organizations to document milestone events and timeless portraits.

My work has been featured in the Diamondback, Terrapin Sports Central, and University of Maryland Athletics Department.
