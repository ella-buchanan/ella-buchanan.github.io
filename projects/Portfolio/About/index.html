HOW IT STARTED

From the Field to behind the lens.

Sports have been part of my life since I was young. I played softball in high school, but eventually, I discovered that I loved capturing the action even more being part of the game.

Photographing sports opened the door to a new creative outlet that combined my passion for sports with my love for storytelling. I started building my skills, taking on new opportunities, and turning my passion into Ella B Images LLC.

Today, I photograph sports, events, and portraits, always looking for the moments that tell a story.
